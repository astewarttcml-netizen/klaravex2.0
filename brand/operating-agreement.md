## ARTICLE V — ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**5.1 Allocations.** All profits and losses of the Company shall be allocated entirely to the
Member.

**5.2 Distributions.** Distributions of cash or other assets shall be made to the Member at
such times and in such amounts as the Member determines in their sole discretion, subject to:

  (a) retention of sufficient funds for Company operations and obligations;

  (b) applicable restrictions under the Act (no distribution may be made if, after giving
  effect to the distribution, the Company would be unable to pay its debts as they become due
  in the ordinary course of business).

**5.3 No Distribution in Kind.** The Company shall not make a distribution in kind without
the consent of the Member.

---

## ARTICLE VI — MANAGEMENT

**6.1 Member-Managed.** The Company shall be member-managed. The Member shall have full,
exclusive, and complete authority to manage and control the business and affairs of the
Company.

**6.2 Authority of Member.** Without limiting Section 6.1, the Member is authorised to:

  (a) execute contracts, agreements, and instruments on behalf of the Company;

  (b) open, maintain, and close bank accounts and investment accounts;

  (c) borrow money and incur indebtedness on behalf of the Company;

  (d) hire, supervise, and terminate employees, contractors, and agents;

  (e) acquire, hold, lease, and dispose of real and personal property;

  (f) institute, prosecute, and settle legal proceedings;

  (g) make all decisions relating to Company operations, strategy, and finances.

**6.3 Delegation.** The Member may appoint officers, managers, or agents and delegate
authority to them. Any such delegation may be revoked at any time.

**6.4 Officers.** The Member may designate the following officers with corresponding
authority:

  - **President / CEO** — general management authority, binds the Company on ordinary
    business matters
  - **Secretary** — maintains records, executes documents as directed by Member
  - **Treasurer / CFO** — financial oversight, banking authority

  The Member may hold all officer titles simultaneously.

**6.5 Decisions.** As the sole Member, all decisions requiring Member approval are made by
the Member alone. No meetings, votes, quorum, or notice requirements apply.

---

## ARTICLE VII — COMPENSATION AND EXPENSES

**7.1 Member Compensation.** The Member is not entitled to compensation for services
rendered to the Company solely by virtue of being the Member. The Member may receive
reasonable compensation for services rendered in an officer or managerial capacity, as
determined by the Member.

**7.2 Expense Reimbursement.** The Company shall reimburse the Member for all reasonable and
documented expenses incurred on behalf of the Company.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP INTEREST

**8.1 Transfer Restrictions.** The Member may transfer all or any portion of their
membership interest at any time, subject to:

  (a) applicable securities laws;

  (b) the transferee executing a written joinder to this Agreement as a condition of admission
  as a substituted Member.

**8.2 Effect of Transfer.** A transferee of a membership interest who is not admitted as a
substituted Member is entitled only to receive distributions and allocations attributable to
the transferred interest, and has no right to participate in management or inspect Company
records.

---

## ARTICLE IX — TAX MATTERS

**9.1 Tax Classification.** The Company shall be treated as a disregarded entity for US
federal income tax purposes (single-member LLC default). All income, deductions, gains,
losses, and credits of the Company shall be reported on the Member's individual US federal
income tax return.

**9.2 S-Corporation Election.** The Member may elect to treat the Company as an S-Corporation
for US federal income tax purposes by timely filing IRS Form 2553. Such election shall be
made only after consultation with a qualified US tax advisor.

**9.3 Tax Returns.** The Member shall cause the Company to prepare or arrange for the
preparation of all required federal, state, and local tax returns and filings.

**9.4 Tax Matters Representative.** The Member is designated as the Tax Matters
Representative of the Company for all federal, state, and local tax purposes.

**9.5 Cross-Border Note.** The Member acknowledges that as a US citizen residing outside the
United States, additional tax obligations may apply, including but not limited to:
US federal income tax on worldwide income, obligations under the US-Germany income tax treaty,
and potential German tax obligations on Company income. The Member shall obtain qualified
US expat tax advice before the Company generates revenue.

---

## ARTICLE X — BOOKS, RECORDS, AND FISCAL YEAR

**10.1 Books and Records.** The Company shall maintain complete and accurate books of account
and other Company records. The Member shall have access to all books and records at all times.

**10.2 Accounting Method.** The Company shall use the cash method of accounting unless
otherwise determined by the Member.

**10.3 Fiscal Year.** The fiscal year of the Company shall be the calendar year (January 1
through December 31).

**10.4 Bank Accounts.** The Company shall maintain one or more bank accounts in the name of
the Company, separate from the Member's personal accounts. No commingling of Company and
personal funds is permitted.

---

## ARTICLE XI — LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

**11.1 Limitation of Liability.** The Member shall not be personally liable for any debt,
obligation, or liability of the Company solely by reason of being the Member, except as
otherwise required by the Act or applicable law.

**11.2 Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Member
and any officer or agent of the Company from and against any claim, liability, damage, cost,
or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from the conduct of Company
business, provided that:

  (a) the Member, officer, or agent acted in good faith and in a manner reasonably believed
  to be in the best interests of the Company; and

  (b) the liability does not arise from fraud, wilful misconduct, or gross negligence.

**11.3 Insurance.** The Company shall obtain and maintain errors and omissions (E&O)
insurance with minimum coverage of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate prior
to entering into any compliance advisory engagement (CMMC, HIPAA, SOC 2, or similar).

---

## ARTICLE XII — COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS

**12.1 Scope Limitations.** The Company provides readiness advisory and preparation services
only. The Company does not conduct CMMC assessments, HIPAA audits, or SOC 2 audits.
All engagement letters and Statements of Work shall explicitly define scope limitations.

**12.2 Marketing Compliance.** All marketing materials shall use the terms "readiness,"
"preparation," or "advisory" when referring to CMMC, HIPAA, or SOC 2 services. The word
"compliance" shall not be used as a service descriptor in marketing without legal review.

**12.3 ITAR.** The Company shall obtain a written ITAR opinion letter from a licensed
export controls attorney before marketing to or engaging any client in the US defense
industrial base or any client handling Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

---

## ARTICLE XIII — DISSOLUTION AND WINDING UP

**13.1 Events of Dissolution.** The Company shall be dissolved upon:

  (a) the written decision of the Member to dissolve;

  (b) the occurrence of any event that makes it unlawful for the Company's business to be
  continued; or

  (c) entry of a judicial decree of dissolution under the Act.

**13.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Member (or a liquidating trustee if the Member
is unavailable) shall wind up the Company's affairs, including:

  (a) satisfying all Company liabilities and obligations;

  (b) distributing remaining assets to the Member.

**13.3 Certificate of Dissolution.** Upon completion of winding up, the Company shall file
a Certificate of Dissolution with the Wyoming Secretary of State.

---

## ARTICLE XIV — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**14.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
the laws of the State of Wyoming, without regard to conflict of law principles.

**14.2 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement of the Member with
respect to the Company and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and negotiations.

**14.3 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the
Member.

**14.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or
unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**14.5 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall
be deemed an original.

**14.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Agreement is intended to confer any
rights or remedies on any person other than the Member and the Company.

**14.7 Headings.** Section headings are for convenience only and shall not affect the
interpretation of this Agreement.
