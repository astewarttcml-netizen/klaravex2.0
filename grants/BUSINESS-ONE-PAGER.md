## What we do
Klaravex is an AI-native managed services provider (MSP). Our platform of **78
production AI agents** (Claude API · FastAPI · Celery · MCP · RAG) handles first-line
IT support, lead qualification, scheduling, and compliance workflows end-to-end —
while a senior engineer owns every outcome. The promise on our homepage: *89% of IT
issues resolved before you finish your coffee* — always AI-labeled, never pretending
to be human, with human-in-the-loop gates on higher-risk actions.

## The problem
Small businesses face enterprise-grade threats (ransomware, compliance, cyber-insurance
requirements) with consumer-grade budgets. Traditional MSPs price them out or
under-serve them; 10–50-person companies routinely have no security posture at all.
Consumers and job-seekers have nowhere trustworthy to take everyday tech problems.

## The model
- **B2B managed plans** (per-seat recurring: Foundation / Assurance / Directive) +
  fixed-fee projects (audits, M365/Azure, compliance readiness).
- **Consumer services** (per-session and packages: tech help, privacy & security,
  job-hunt tech kit, AI-skills coaching) via Stripe self-checkout.
- AI deflection is the unit-economics engine: agents resolve routine tickets at
  near-zero marginal cost, so one senior engineer can serve a client base that
  traditionally needs a 10-person MSP.
