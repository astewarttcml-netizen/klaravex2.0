# MDR Partner Outreach — Huntress (primary) & Blackpoint (backup)
**Prepared:** 2026-06-03 · Goal: open a channel/partner account so Klaravex can resell managed security at margin.

---

## Before you send — what they'll ask
Both run formal MSP/partner programs. Have ready:
- **Business identity:** Klaravex LLC (Wyoming), EIN, klaravex.com, US business address/phone.
- **Honest stage:** new/launching MSP — say so. Channel-first vendors (esp. Huntress) actively recruit early-stage partners; you don't need an existing book of business.
- **Pipeline framing:** "onboarding first clients in healthcare-adjacent, legal, and financial SMBs."
- **Volume note:** Huntress has a 50-endpoint minimum — you meet it by **pooling across clients**, not per-client. Say you'll hit it within your first few engagements.
- **No financials/credit needed** for a channel account (unlike Net-30 vendors).

> Sequence: Huntress first (lowest cost, broadest stack, channel-first). Open Blackpoint only if/when a client needs top-tier response.

>
> Managed EDR + ITDR with a real human SOC is core to our offering, and Huntress is my first-choice security backbone specifically because you're channel-first and protect partner margin. I'm onboarding my first clients now and expect to clear the 50-endpoint threshold across them shortly.
>
> Could we set up a partner account and review MSP pricing? I'd also like to understand ITDR, SAT, and SIEM bundling, and your onboarding/deployment process.
>
> Thanks,
> Anthony — Klaravex LLC · [phone] · klaravex.com

**On the call, confirm:** per-endpoint MSP price + volume tiers (50/100/250/500/1000), whether 24/7 SOC is included (it is), billing in arrears / no annual lock, deployment via your RMM (Atera), and co-branding for client-facing reports.
