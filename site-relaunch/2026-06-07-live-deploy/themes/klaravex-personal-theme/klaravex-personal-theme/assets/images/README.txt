KLARAVEX PERSONAL THEME — PHOTO GUIDE
=======================================

REQUIRED
─────────────────────────────────────────────
1. hero-bg.jpg
   Size: 1600 × 1000px minimum
   Style: Warm, natural, relatable.
     Options that work:
     - Someone helping an older parent with a laptop at a kitchen table
     - Person on a sofa with a laptop, warm living room light
     - Two people looking at a screen together, smiling
     AVOID: Server rooms, suits, stock handshakes, blue LED lighting
   Default fallback: Unsplash photo-1531746790731-6c087fecd65a

HOW TO SET YOUR PHOTO
─────────────────────────────────────────────
WordPress Admin > Appearance > Customize
> Klaravex Personal > Hero > Background photo
