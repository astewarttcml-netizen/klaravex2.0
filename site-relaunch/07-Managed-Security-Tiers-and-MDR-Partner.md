# PART 3 — MDR / EDR White-Label Partner Comparison

**You will NOT build a SOC.** You white-label one, layer Loki on top for client-facing triage, and resell at margin. This is standard lean-MSP practice and the only sane path for a solo/lean operator.

| Option | Cost to you (MSP) | What it is | Fit for Klaravex |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Huntress** (Managed EDR + ITDR + SAT + SIEM) | **~$2.50–$3.50/endpoint/mo** (channel; ~$1.95 at high volume); 50-unit min (pool across clients) | Channel-first, 24/7 human SOC **included**, easy deploy, protects partner margin, broad SMB stack (EDR, identity, training, SIEM) | ⭐ **Best starting pick.** Lowest entry cost, channel-first (won't compete with you), one vendor covers EDR + identity + awareness training. Resell ~$8.99 retail → healthy margin. |
| **Blackpoint Cyber** | ~$8–15/endpoint/mo | Fastest/most aggressive response, "serious" SOC, pairs with Defender | ✅ Upgrade option when a client needs top-tier response; higher cost compresses margin |
| **Microsoft Defender for Business** | ~$3/user/mo (often already in M365 BP) | The **EDR layer**, not MDR — no human SOC | ✅ Use as the EDR engine for M365-heavy clients, then layer Huntress or ConnectWise MDR on top |
| **ConnectWise MDR** | varies | MDR that runs on top of Defender/Bitdefender/SentinelOne | ◽ Viable if you standardize on the ConnectWise stack; more platform lock-in |
| **SentinelOne** | enterprise pricing | Excellent EDR, but sells direct-to-enterprise | ❌ Channel economics don't fit a lean MSP — skip for now |

### Recommendation
1. **Sign with Huntress as your security backbone.** One vendor gives you Managed EDR + identity threat detection + security awareness training + 24/7 SOC, at the lowest entry cost, channel-first so it never competes with you. The 50-endpoint minimum is met by pooling across your client base.
2. **Use Microsoft Defender for Business as the EDR engine** for M365-heavy clients (often already licensed) and let Huntress MDR monitor it — best margin, least new tooling.
3. **Keep Blackpoint in your back pocket** for clients who demand the most aggressive response and will pay for it.
4. **Loki sits in front of all of it** — client-facing triage, plain-English alerts, escalation. That's the Klaravex differentiator the white-label SOC can't offer on its own.
5. **Margin math:** Huntress ~$3 cost → bundle into Assurance at $15/user effective → strong recurring margin while still under the market's $225–350 premium band. The security lines are what move Foundation→Assurance and lift per-user revenue without you adding labor.
