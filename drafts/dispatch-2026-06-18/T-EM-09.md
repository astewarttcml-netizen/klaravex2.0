# **Service Offering Documentation**  
**Task ID:** T-EM-09 (Priority: High, Type: Design)  
**Title:** Founder OPSEC / Formation Privacy as a Service  

## **1. Overview**  
This service provides end-to-end operational security (OPSEC) and privacy solutions for solo founders during and after business formation. It mitigates exposure risks inherent in public filings, registries, and third-party data brokers while ensuring compliance and operational continuity.  

## **2. Service Components**  

### **A. Pre-Formation Advisory**  
- **Registered Agent (RA) Selection:** Guidance on privacy-focused RA providers to avoid personal address exposure.  
- **Virtual Mailbox Setup:** Secure mail handling with commercial addresses (non-RA) for public filings.  
- **Business Phone Routing:** VoIP or forwarding solutions to prevent personal number leaks.  
- **EIN Routing:** IRS-compliant structuring to minimize linkage to personal identifiers.  

### **B. Post-Formation Remediation**  
- **USPTO TEAS Change of Correspondence:** Updates USPTO filings to remove personal addresses.  
- **Wyoming SOS Amended Filing:** Submits corrections to replace personal details with service addresses.  
- **Data Broker Opt-Outs:** Automated/manual removal from:  
  - DUNS (Dun & Bradstreet)  
  - Hoovers  
  - Experian Business  
- **WHOIS Audit:** Scans and redacts personal info from domain registrations.  
- **Virtual Office Migration:** Transitions from personal/home addresses to professional virtual offices.  

### **C. Ongoing Monitoring**  
- Quarterly audits of public records (SOS, USPTO, WHOIS).  
- Alerts for new exposures (e.g., accidental personal address filings).  
- Renewal management for virtual office/mailbox services.  

## **3. Deliverables**  
- **Pre-formation checklist** (tailored to jurisdiction).  
- **Post-formation remediation report** (detailing completed opt-outs/filings).  
- **Monitoring dashboard** (summary of active protections, exposure status).  

## **4. Pricing & Engagement**  
- **Tier 1 (Basic):** Pre-formation advisory + post-formation remediation ($1,500 one-time).  
- **Tier 2 (Comprehensive):** Includes ongoing monitoring ($300/month, 12-month min).  

## **5. Compliance Notes**  
- All actions adhere to state/federal business registration requirements.  
- Client retains legal ownership of all filings; we act as a facilitator.
