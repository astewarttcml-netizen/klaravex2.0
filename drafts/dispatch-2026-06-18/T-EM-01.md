**Last Updated:** [Insert Date]

## **Objective**  
Evaluate third-party data broker removal partner programs for integration with Klaravex, focusing on:  
- **Reseller/Whitelabel Terms**  
- **Cost Structure (Per-Seat vs. Retail Pricing)**  
- **Broker Coverage (200 vs. 500+)**  
- **API Availability for MSP Automation**  
- **Reporting Quality for Client Deliverables**  
- **EU/GDPR Opt-Out Compliance (klaravex.com)**

## **Key Findings**  

### **1. Reseller & Whitelabel Flexibility**  
- **Optery & Privacy Bee** offer full white-labeling, ideal for Klaravex branding.  
- **OneRep** is co-branded, which may dilute client perception.  
- **Kanary** lacks true reseller options (referral-only).  

### **2. Cost Efficiency**  
- **Optery & OneRep** provide per-seat pricing with volume discounts (~$3–$8/user/month at scale).  
- **Privacy Bee** has a higher flat fee but includes premium features.  
- **Kanary** is retail-only, making it cost-prohibitive for MSPs.  

### **3. Broker Coverage**  
- **Optery (500+)** and **Privacy Bee (400+)** lead in coverage.  
- **OneRep (200+)** and **Kanary (250+)** lag, missing critical EU/GDPR-focused brokers.  

### **4. Automation & API Support**  
- **Optery’s RESTful API** enables seamless integration with Klaravex workflows.  
- **Privacy Bee** offers limited API support (no bulk processing).  
- **OneRep & Kanary** require manual intervention.  

### **5. Reporting & Client Deliverables**  
- **Optery & Privacy Bee** provide white-label reports suitable for B2B clients.  
- **OneRep’s dashboard** is functional but lacks customization.  
- **Kanary’s email summaries** are insufficient for enterprise use.  

### **6. EU/GDPR Compliance**  
- **Optery & Privacy Bee** fully automate opt-outs for EU brokers (critical for klaravex.com).  
- **OneRep** requires manual submissions for GDPR requests.  
- **Kanary** does not support EU removals.  

---

## **Recommendation**  
**Primary Choice:** **Optery for Business**  
- **Why?** Best coverage (500+ brokers), full API automation, white-label reporting, and GDPR compliance.  

**Secondary Option:** **Privacy Bee Business**  
- **Why?** Strong coverage (400+) and white-labeling, but higher flat fees and limited API.  

**Eliminated:**  
- **OneRep** (Limited coverage, no API, manual GDPR handling).  
- **Kanary** (No reseller program, retail-only pricing).
