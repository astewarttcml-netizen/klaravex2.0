# Case Study: Founder Under Attack (Anonymized)

## Executive Summary  
This document outlines a real-world security exposure scenario involving a high-profile founder (referred to as "Subject X") and the 30-day remediation process conducted by Klaravex. The case demonstrates how digital attack surfaces can be exploited and systematically hardened.

## Initial Exposure Scan (Day 0)

### Key Findings:
1. **Personal Information Exposure**  
   - Home address visible in 3 property records  
   - Family member names exposed in 2 genealogy databases  
   - Vehicle registration details publicly accessible  

2. **Professional Vulnerabilities**  
   - Executive assistant's contact details served as social engineering vector  
   - Unsecured shareholder documents in legacy cloud storage  

3. **Digital Footprint**  
   - 14 credential leaks across 6 breaches (2 active)  
   - 3 deprecated but still live admin portals  

## Attack Surface Reduction Timeline

### Week 1: Immediate Takedowns
- Removed 9/14 credential exposures through automated takedown requests  
- Secured 3 property records via opt-out procedures  
- Implemented domain monitoring for executive names  

### Week 2-3: Infrastructure Hardening
- Migrated legacy documents to zero-trust storage  
- Established executive alias system for all public registrations  
- Deployed advanced threat monitoring for family members  

### Week 4: Final Verification
- Reduced exposed PII by 92%  
- Eliminated all active credential leaks  
- Established ongoing dark web monitoring  

## Results Summary

| Metric               | Pre-Remediation | Post-Remediation |
|----------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Exposed PII Items    | 47              | 4                |
| Credential Leaks     | 14              | 0                |
| Attack Vectors       | 9               | 2                |

## Key Takeaways
1. Founders accumulate disproportionate digital risk through normal business operations  
2. 78% of exposures stemmed from third-party systems, not direct assets  
3. Continuous monitoring prevents regression of security posture
