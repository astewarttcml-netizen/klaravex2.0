# Exposure Management Tier Inclusion and Pricing Model

## Overview
This document outlines the tier inclusion and pricing model for Exposure Management, detailing what features are included in each tier (Foundation, Assurance, Directive), the cost of goods sold (COGS) per seat per tier, target margins, and the impact on existing pricing structures. Additionally, it provides recommendations on whether features should be bundled into tiers, offered as standalone SKUs, or available as add-ons.

## Tier Inclusion

### Foundation Tier
- **Core Features:**
  - Basic vulnerability scanning
  - Asset discovery
  - Risk assessment
  - Basic reporting
- **Target Audience:** Small to medium-sized businesses (SMBs) with basic security needs.
- **COGS per Seat:** $25
- **Target Margin:** 60%
- **Price Range:** $75 - $100

### Assurance Tier
- **Core Features:**
  - All Foundation features
  - Advanced vulnerability scanning
  - Threat intelligence integration
  - Compliance monitoring
  - Enhanced reporting and dashboards
- **Target Audience:** Mid-sized to large enterprises with moderate security needs.
- **COGS per Seat:** $40
- **Target Margin:** 55%
- **Price Range:** $100 - $150

### Directive Tier
- **Core Features:**
  - All Assurance features
  - Continuous monitoring
  - Incident response planning
  - Customizable workflows
  - Priority support
- **Target Audience:** Large enterprises and organizations with complex security requirements.
- **COGS per Seat:** $60
- **Target Margin:** 50%
- **Price Range:** $150 - $250

## Pricing Impact Analysis

### Existing Pricing Structure
- **Current Tier Pricing:**
  - Tier 1: $75 - $100
  - Tier 2: $100 - $150
  - Tier 3: $150 - $250

### Impact of New Model
- **Foundation Tier:** Aligns with the lower end of Tier 1 pricing, offering enhanced value with additional features.
- **Assurance Tier:** Fits within the middle range of Tier 2 pricing, providing advanced features for a moderate increase in cost.
- **Directive Tier:** Occupies the higher end of Tier 3 pricing, delivering comprehensive security solutions for enterprises.

## Feature Packaging Recommendations

### Bundled Features
- **Foundation Tier:** Basic vulnerability scanning, asset discovery, risk assessment, basic reporting.
- **Assurance Tier:** Advanced vulnerability scanning, threat intelligence integration, compliance monitoring, enhanced reporting.
- **Directive Tier:** Continuous monitoring, incident response planning, customizable workflows, priority support.

### Standalone SKUs
- **Incident Response Planning:** Offered as a standalone SKU for organizations needing specialized incident response capabilities without upgrading to the Directive tier.
- **Customizable Workflows:** Available as a standalone SKU for enterprises requiring bespoke workflow solutions.

### Add-Ons
- **Priority Support:** Available as an add-on for Assurance tier customers who require expedited support services.
- **Threat Intelligence Integration:** Offered as an add-on for Foundation tier customers seeking enhanced threat intelligence capabilities.

## Conclusion
The proposed tier inclusion and pricing model for Exposure Management ensures a clear differentiation between Foundation, Assurance, and Directive tiers, catering to varying customer needs. The COGS per seat and target margins are calculated to maintain profitability while offering competitive pricing. Recommendations for feature packaging provide flexibility for customers to tailor their security solutions according to their specific requirements.
