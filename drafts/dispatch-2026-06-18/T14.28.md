## Article 1: Security Basics  
**Title:** What to Do If You Think You've Been Hacked  

### Overview  
This article provides actionable steps for individuals and businesses to take if they suspect a security breach or unauthorized access to their systems.  

### Steps to Take  
1. **Disconnect from the Internet:** Immediately disconnect the affected device(s) from the network to prevent further damage.  
2. **Change Passwords:** Update passwords for all accounts, especially those with administrative privileges.  
3. **Scan for Malware:** Use trusted antivirus software to scan and remove any malicious programs.  
4. **Check for Unusual Activity:** Review account logs, emails, and financial transactions for signs of unauthorized access.  
5. **Notify Relevant Parties:** Inform your IT team, service providers, and, if necessary, law enforcement.  
6. **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Add an extra layer of security to your accounts.  
7. **Backup Data:** Ensure all critical data is backed up securely.  
8. **Seek Professional Help:** Consult cybersecurity experts to assess and mitigate the breach.  

### Prevention Tips  
- Regularly update software and systems.  
- Educate employees on phishing and social engineering tactics.  
- Use strong, unique passwords for all accounts.

## Article 2: M365 & Cloud  
**Title:** M365 Security Defaults Every SMB Should Turn On  

### Overview  
Microsoft 365 (M365) offers robust security features that are often overlooked by small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). This article highlights essential security defaults to enable for enhanced protection.  

### Key Security Defaults  
1. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Require MFA for all users to prevent unauthorized access.  
2. **Conditional Access Policies:** Restrict access based on location, device, and user risk level.  
3. **Data Loss Prevention (DLP):** Configure DLP policies to prevent sensitive data from being shared externally.  
4. **Audit Logging:** Enable audit logs to monitor and investigate suspicious activities.  
5. **Secure Score:** Use Microsoft’s Secure Score to assess and improve your security posture.  
6. **Email Encryption:** Encrypt sensitive emails to protect confidential information.  
7. **Anti-Malware Protection:** Ensure anti-malware tools are enabled and up-to-date.  

### Best Practices  
- Regularly review and update security settings.  
- Train employees on M365 security features.  
- Monitor alerts and take immediate action on potential threats.

## Article 3: Business IT  
**Title:** The Realistic IT Budget for a 25-Person Regulated SMB  

### Overview  
This article outlines a practical IT budget for a 25-person small or medium-sized business (SMB) operating in a regulated industry, such as healthcare or finance.  

### Budget Breakdown  
1. **Hardware (20-25%):**  
   - Workstations, laptops, and peripherals.  
   - Servers and networking equipment.  
2. **Software (15-20%):**  
   - Licensing for operating systems, productivity tools, and specialized software.  
3. **Cybersecurity (20-25%):**  
   - Antivirus, firewalls, and endpoint protection.  
   - Security training and compliance tools.  
4. **Cloud Services (10-15%):**  
   - Cloud storage, backup, and SaaS subscriptions.  
5. **IT Support (15-20%):**  
   - Managed IT services or in-house IT staff.  
6. **Miscellaneous (5-10%):**  
   - Contingency funds for unexpected expenses.  

### Tips for Cost Optimization  
- Leverage open-source software where feasible.  
- Negotiate volume licensing agreements.  
- Regularly audit IT expenses to identify savings opportunities.

## Article 4: IT Readiness  
**Title:** What HIPAA Actually Expects from a 10-Person Medical Practice  

### Overview  
This article clarifies the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) requirements for small medical practices, focusing on practical steps for compliance.  

### Key Requirements  
1. **Risk Assessment:** Conduct regular risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities.  
2. **Policies and Procedures:** Develop and document HIPAA-compliant policies.  
3. **Employee Training:** Train staff on HIPAA rules and data handling practices.  
4. **Access Controls:** Implement role-based access to protect patient information.  
5. **Data Encryption:** Encrypt electronic protected health information (ePHI) in transit and at rest.  
6. **Breach Notification:** Establish procedures for reporting breaches within the required timeframe.  
7. **Business Associate Agreements (BAAs):** Ensure BAAs are in place with third-party vendors handling ePHI.  

### Simplified Compliance Checklist  
- Use HIPAA-compliant software and tools.  
- Regularly update security measures.  
- Maintain thorough documentation of compliance efforts.
