## Company Overview

Klaravex is a US-based **AI-powered managed IT and security** partner for lean
SMBs. Klaravex AI resolves first-line work 24/7; a named senior engineer owns
the hard cases. We do **not** take vendor commissions. We do **not** staff
junior ticket queues.

We deliver the full catalog on klaravex.com: **four pillars / fifteen
services** (network & security, strategy & vCIO, cloud & productivity,
infrastructure & support), plus packaged **Foundation / Assurance /
Directive** plans, MDR, and readiness / vCISO advisory.

Entity: Klaravex LLC (Wyoming). Coverage: nationwide US, remote-first.

## Voice & Tone

- Corporate **“we” / “Klaravex”** only — never personal names, founder bio,
  or first-person singular (“I”, “me”, “my”).
- Never the word **“Loki”** — say **Klaravex AI** or “our AI support
  coordinator”.
- Professional, direct, senior — no agency fluff.
- Lead with concrete numbers and outcomes when available.
- Platform-agnostic confidence: recommend the right stack for the client.

### Language rubric (marketing copy)

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|--------|
| readiness, advisory, audit-ready, evidence | **compliance** (as marketing noun) |
| Foundation / Assurance / Directive + exact $ | Invented tiers or other prices |
| Klaravex AI | Loki, unnamed “chatbot” |
| klaravex.com / personal.klaravex.com CTAs | Soft CTAs with no URL |
| readiness review / assessment | Empty “digital transformation”, “synergy” |

Also banned in marketing drafts:

- Defense / DIB / CMMC targeting or content
- Infrastructure vendor names that are **ops-only** (Hetzner, Azure *as our
  host*, Atera, Vapi, Smartlead, Apollo) on **consumer** drafts
- Selling hardware on commission narratives

## Communication Guidelines

- Active voice; short sentences; one job per section.
- Name the vertical when writing B2B (law / accounting / medical / etc.).
- Every marketing draft ends with a CTA to **klaravex.com** or
  **personal.klaravex.com** (with UTMs when the stream provides a pattern).
- When mentioning network gear: pair **enterprise firewall** (Palo Alto /
  FortiGate / Cisco) with **UniFi** only when the audience is SMB LAN — don’t
  imply Klaravex “is a UniFi shop” exclusively.
