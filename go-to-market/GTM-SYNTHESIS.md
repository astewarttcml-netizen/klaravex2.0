# Klaravex — Go-To-Market Synthesis (Design · Marketing · Engineering · Finance)
Date: 2026-06-08 · Source: 4 agency specialist agents

## The one-line thesis (all four agree)
Klaravex delivers **MSP-grade managed IT at solo-operator cost** because agentic AI (Klara) does the work and certified engineers own the outcome. The whole business rests on ONE number: **% of tickets Klara resolves without a human (AI deflection rate)** — it's the margin lever, the scale lever, and the moat.

## Where all 4 specialists CONVERGED (do these — high confidence)
1. **Focus B2B first** (US klaravex.com + Berlin klaravex.com). 10–50x the LTV of B2C, same Klara engine. B2C = passive SEO/inbound capture, not paid acquisition yet. (Marketing + Finance)
2. **Fix the pricing: launch a true B2B per-seat "Managed" anchor plan ~$49/seat/mo (€49) with a ~$245/mo floor.** The current $24/mo "Home Membership" on a B2B site is the single biggest revenue leak. Convert every one-time/incident buyer into recurring. (Finance — "#1 highest-ROI move")
3. **Partners/referrals are the #1 channel** for a lean budget: Berlin Steuerberater, US bookkeepers/fractional CFOs, cyber-insurance brokers. Then AEO/SEO. Paid search LAST, only on proven intent. (Marketing)
4. **The free 45-min Assessment is the universal top-of-funnel hook** — make the deliverable a concrete red/yellow/green risk scorecard that sells itself. (Marketing + Design)
5. **Klara chat widget = the primary conversion + qualification surface** (proactive, quick-reply CTAs, qualifies & books, hot/warm/cold tag to CRM). (Marketing + Design)
6. **Deploy the "Human-Accountability Cue"** ("Klara handles it. A certified engineer owns it.") + always-labeled AI/human across all 4 sites — resolves the US "AI-first" vs EU "human-led" tension and is the #1 trust/conversion lift. (Design)
7. **Engineering: kill the single-server SPOF first** (split Postgres + WAL backups + tested restore), then build the **eval + observability harness** that measures/improves the deflection rate, on **RLS tenant isolation** + **region cells** (US/EU/future SA) for GDPR. (Engineering)
8. **Bootstrap.** 75–80% gross margins, breakeven ~Month 4–6, MRR self-funds. Only real capital need: a ~$30–60k working-capital buffer. The ceiling to $1M ARR is your time, not money. (Finance)

## North-Star + guardrail metrics
- **North Star:** AI deflection rate (target ≥75%). If it drops to 60%, margins compress ~10pts and breakeven slips 2–3 months.
- Net New MRR · one-time→recurring conversion % · churn (B2B<3%, B2C<6%) · LTV:CAC (target ≥4:1) · tickets/customer/mo (margin tripwire >5).

## Year-1 targets (base case)
- 80–120 active B2B clients (US+EU) · **MRR $25–40k** · ~$576k ARR run-rate by M12 · $1M ARR ~M18–20 bootstrapped.
