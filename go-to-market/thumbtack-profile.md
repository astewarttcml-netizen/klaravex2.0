## Business Description

Running a business is hard enough without tech problems slowing you down. At Klaravex, we handle everything IT — from setting up your office network to keeping your data secure — so you can focus on what you actually do.

We work with small and mid-size businesses across every US state, all delivered remotely. No waiting around for a technician to show up. Most issues are resolved same-day, and our AI assistant Loki is available around the clock to help your team with everyday IT questions, password resets, connectivity issues, and more — even at 2am on a Sunday.

**Who we help:**
We work best with businesses of 5 to 150 people, especially medical and dental offices, law firms, financial advisory practices, and general service businesses that rely on computers, email, and cloud tools to run.

**What we do:**
- Keep your computers, email, and software running and up to date
- Protect your business from hackers, ransomware, and data breaches
- Set up and manage your office network, including WiFi and firewalls
- Administer Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace
- Run cybersecurity checkups so you know where your risks are
- Provide your team with a helpdesk they can actually reach, 24/7

**What makes us different:**
Our AI assistant Loki handles first-line support instantly, 24 hours a day, so your team never waits. For anything more complex, a human expert steps in fast.

We also keep it plain English. No tech-speak, no confusing invoices, no upsells you did not ask for. Flat monthly rates or fixed project prices, always agreed upfront.

**What to expect:**
We start with a short discovery call — about 20 minutes — to understand your setup and your pain points. From there, we put together a clear plan and quote. Most engagements start within a week.

## FAQs

**Q: Do you work on-site or remotely?**
We work remotely, which means faster response times and lower costs. The vast majority of IT issues can be handled entirely over the internet. For physical networking equipment, we ship hardware directly with clear setup guidance. We serve clients across all 50 states.

**Q: What size businesses do you work with?**
We work best with businesses between 5 and 150 employees — single-office teams, multi-location practices, and remote-first companies. Designed specifically for businesses that do not have a full-time IT department but still need professional-grade support.

**Q: How quickly do you respond when something goes wrong?**
Our AI assistant Loki responds instantly, around the clock, for everyday issues. For complex problems needing a human, we respond within a few hours during business hours. Managed IT clients get priority response and a dedicated point of contact.

**Q: Do I have to sign a long contract?**
No. Project work is quoted and billed per project, no ongoing commitment. Managed IT services run on monthly agreements with a short initial term (typically 3 months) — no multi-year lock-in.

**Q: We already have someone who handles IT occasionally. Can you work alongside them?**
Yes, this is a common situation. We work well alongside office managers or part-time IT helpers. We can fill specific gaps — like being the 24/7 helpdesk, handling M365 administration, or running a security assessment — without replacing the person you already trust.
