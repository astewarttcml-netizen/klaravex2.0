## Agency Overview / Bio

**Most IT vendors make you wait. Klaravex doesn't.**

When your team hits a problem at 7am before a client call, or at 9pm before a board presentation, you shouldn't be leaving a voicemail or waiting in a queue. Klaravex is built differently: our AI support agent, Loki, handles first-line IT and security issues around the clock — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — with response times measured in seconds, not business days.

That is not a chatbot. Loki is a purpose-built AI operations layer that triages issues, walks users through fixes, escalates intelligently, and closes tickets without our human principal having to touch every routine request. The result: your team gets immediate, competent help at any hour, and complex issues get a senior practitioner's attention instead of being buried under tier-1 noise.

**Why this matters for your business:**

Traditional MSPs charge per technician. That means their incentive is to keep tickets open and staff billable. Klaravex runs on fixed-fee engagements. Our incentive is to resolve issues fast, prevent them from recurring, and get Loki handling as much of your day-to-day as possible — so your per-user cost stays flat even as your team grows.

**What we do:**

Klaravex manages the full IT and security stack for small and mid-sized businesses across the United States. Our core work covers:

- **Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace** — tenant configuration, user lifecycle management, security hardening, email deliverability, licensing optimization, and migrations.
- **AWS cloud management** — account security posture, IAM hygiene, cost governance, and workload monitoring.
- **Network infrastructure** — Ubiquiti UniFi firewall, switching, and wireless deployments and ongoing management.
- **Managed security** — endpoint protection, Microsoft Secure Score remediation, identity and access management, MFA enforcement, conditional access policies, and security awareness training.
- **HIPAA-adjacent readiness** — for medical practices, health tech companies, and adjacent businesses that handle PHI or work with covered entities.
- **24/7 AI-powered helpdesk** — Loki handles password resets, MFA issues, device onboarding, software troubleshooting, and dozens of other routine requests without human intervention.

**Who we work with:**

SMBs with 10 to 150 users who are past the "my nephew handles IT" phase. Healthcare-adjacent companies, legal practices, financial advisory firms, and any business on M365 or Google Workspace that is serious about security.

All services are delivered fully remotely. We respond to all messages within a few hours during business hours, and Loki is available immediately for any client who integrates our support channel.

If you are a business owner or operations manager who is tired of unresponsive IT vendors, vague invoices, and tickets that sit open for days — this is the profile you were looking for.

## Service Offerings

### 1. Microsoft 365 Setup, Migration & Security Hardening — $400–$2,500
Whether you are moving from Google Workspace, migrating from on-premises Exchange, or starting a new M365 tenant from scratch, we handle the full configuration — not just the data move. Every engagement includes: tenant security baseline (Secure Score remediation), MFA enforcement, Conditional Access policy configuration, email deliverability (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), mailbox migration with zero-downtime cutover planning, user onboarding, and licensing review. All work is scoped and fixed-fee before we begin.

### 2. SMB Cybersecurity Assessment — Posture Review and Remediation Roadmap — $750–$2,000
A practical, outcomes-focused assessment — not a compliance checkbox exercise. We review M365 or Google Workspace security configuration, endpoint protection, identity and access management, network security, backup and recovery posture, and phishing/social engineering exposure. Output: written report with plain-English executive summary, technical findings by severity, and a prioritized remediation roadmap. Designed for healthcare-adjacent, legal, and financial firms.

### 3. Google Workspace Setup, Configuration & Administration — $300–$1,500
Full Google Workspace deployment and hardening: tenant setup or audit, security baseline, 2-step verification enforcement, email deliverability, group and alias structure, shared drive organization, user lifecycle documentation. Admin retainer available from $75/user/month.

### 4. Ubiquiti UniFi Network Deployment & Remote Management — $400–$2,000
VLAN segmentation, guest network isolation, firewall configuration, switch and wireless AP setup, remote management console. Remote monitoring included in all managed service tiers. Ideal for office build-outs, clinics, and SMBs needing enterprise-grade networking.

### 5. Fully Managed IT and Security — $75–$150/user/month
Fixed monthly fee, all-inclusive: 24/7 AI helpdesk (Loki), endpoint monitoring and patch management, M365 or Google Workspace administration, Ubiquiti UniFi monitoring, monthly security posture review, dedicated escalation point of contact. Minimum 10 users.
