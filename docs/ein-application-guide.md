## Step-by-Step on the IRS Site

1. Go to the URL above — click **"Apply Online Now"**
2. Read the disclaimer, click **"Begin Application"**
3. Entity type → **Limited Liability Company**
4. LLC questions:
   - How many members? → **1**
   - State where LLC was organized? → **Wyoming**
5. Reason for applying → **Started a new business**
6. Responsible party screen:
   - Enter your **full legal name** as it appears on your SSN card
   - Enter your **SSN** (not an ITIN — you have a SSN)
7. Business address:
   - Use your **Wyoming registered agent address** or a US address you own/control
   - Do NOT use a German address — IRS requires a US address for online filing
8. Business details:
   - Business legal name: **Klaravex LLC**
   - Trade name / DBA: leave blank (unless you want one)
   - County and state of principal business: use the state matching your address
9. Business activity:
   - Category: **Other**
   - Specific activity: type **"Managed IT services and cybersecurity advisory"**
10. Employees:
    - Do you expect to have employees in the next 12 months? → **No** (unless you plan to hire immediately)
    - If no: confirm you are applying for banking / tax purposes
11. Review everything → **Submit**
12. **EIN confirmation page appears immediately** — print it or screenshot it NOW

## Notes

- The IRS online application is only available **7am–10pm ET Monday–Friday**. If the session times out, you have to start over — do not walk away mid-application.
- If the online system rejects your application (rare for US citizens with SSNs), you can call the IRS Business & Specialty Tax Line: **1-800-829-4933**, Monday–Friday 7am–7pm local time. Have the same information above ready. They will give you the EIN over the phone.
- Do not pay any third-party service for an EIN. The IRS provides it free.
- The EIN confirmation letter (CP 575) will arrive by mail in 4–6 weeks. The online confirmation is sufficient for opening bank accounts — you do not need to wait for the letter.
