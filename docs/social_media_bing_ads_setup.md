# Bing Ads Setup

## Status: Cancelled

This task has been cancelled as part of the decision to outsource social media management to a third-party company. 

## Reasoning
After evaluating the need for Bing Ads integration, we decided that:
1. Social media management is better handled by specialized third-party experts
2. Internal resources should be focused on core business operations
3. The outsourcing approach will provide better results and cost efficiency
