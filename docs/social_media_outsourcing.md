# Social Media Management Outsourcing Decision

## Overview
We have decided to outsource our social media campaign management to a third-party company. This decision was made to leverage specialized expertise and focus our internal resources on core business operations.

## Rationale
- Access to specialized social media marketing expertise
- Reduced internal resource allocation for social media management
- Professional campaign optimization and analytics
- Focus on core business development

## Next Steps
1. Coordinate with third-party company for campaign setup
2. Ensure proper handover of existing social media assets
3. Set up proper tracking and analytics with the third-party
4. Establish communication protocols for campaign updates

## Implementation Plan
Since we're outsourcing, we need to:
- Remove Bing Ads integration from the system (this task was cancelled)
- Clean up social media task definitions 
- Update documentation
- Remove unused dependencies
