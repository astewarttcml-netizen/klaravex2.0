## Klaravex Competitive Advantages

### Structural advantages (hard to copy)
1. **AI-native delivery** — Loki is the product, not a feature. Competitors add AI on top of human-first operations; Klaravex builds human expertise on top of AI-first operations. Fundamentally lower cost structure.
2. **24/7 availability without overnight staffing** — AI handles first contact at 3am. No competitor at this price point does this.
3. **Transparent AI** — every competitor either hides AI use or positions it as consulting. Klaravex leads with it. This is a trust differentiator in an era of AI skepticism.
4. **Consumer + B2B under one roof** — cross-sell path from consumer client to their small business that no MSP competitor has. HelloTech can't sell compliance advisory; Ntiva won't take a residential client.
5. **Multi-cloud neutrality** — M365 + GWorkspace + AWS in one practice. Most MSPs are M365-heavy and treat AWS/GWorkspace as afterthoughts.

### Pricing advantages
| Tier | Klaravex | Ntiva (est.) | Corsica (est.) |
|------|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Foundation/entry | ~$75–100/user/mo | ~$150–200 | ~$150+ |
| Compliance/advisory | ~$150–250/user/mo | ~$250–300 | Custom |
| Consumer | $19–29/mo | N/A | N/A |

Klaravex can price lower because AI reduces per-ticket labor cost — not because quality is lower.

## Win Themes (use in all sales conversations)

1. **"Your issues resolved before your MSP's ticket gets assigned"** — 24/7 AI first contact vs next-business-day human routing
2. **"Enterprise-grade support at SMB prices"** — AI leverage = lower cost, not lower quality
3. **"We tell you when AI handled it and when a human did"** — trust through transparency, not trust through illusion
4. **"One partner, three clouds"** — M365, Google Workspace, and AWS without switching vendors
5. **"Your IT support also understands HIPAA"** — compliance-fluent from day one, not bolted on

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|---------|
| "We already have an MSP" | What's their average ticket resolution time? Klaravex resolves common issues instantly, 24/7. |
| "We're not sure about AI handling our IT" | You already use AI in your email, calendar, and search. Klaravex tells you when AI handled something — most vendors don't. |
| "You're too small / just started" | You get the senior expert directly — no account managers, no tier-1 call centers. And Loki never calls in sick. |
| "Your price seems low" | AI handles first-line volume. Human expertise costs what it costs — you're not subsidising a bench of junior techs. |
| "We need on-site support" | Most IT issues are resolved remotely faster than a tech can drive to your office. For hardware failures, we coordinate with local resources. |

## Business Listings Plan

**B2B directories**

|-------------|------|-------|
| CompTIA | ~$900+/yr | Partner Locator used by some SMB buyers; credibility signal |
| MSPAlliance | Contact for price | Public member directory; better for peer/vendor relationships than leads |

### Defer until revenue

- **HIMSS corporate membership** (~$2,500–5,000/yr) — only worth it at scale targeting hospitals
- **Clutch sponsored placement** (~$1,500–2,000+/yr) — upgrade from free after 2–3 verified reviews
- **BBB accreditation** (~$400–500/yr) — credibility signal for older demographics only

### Review collection strategy

Clutch, GoodFirms, G2, and UpCity all require verified client reviews to rank. The strategy:
1. Get 3–5 consumer clients in first 60 days (easier to ask for reviews than B2B)
2. Ask every satisfied client for a review on Clutch + Google simultaneously
3. Once 2–3 reviews exist on Clutch, evaluate paid placement ROI
