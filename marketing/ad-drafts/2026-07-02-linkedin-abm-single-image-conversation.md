## Part 6 — Image creative brief (for designer)

Hand this to the designer along with `brand/brand-strategy.md`:

- **Palette:** Klaravex primary teal + white + charcoal. Use the exact HEX from brand guidelines.
- **Typography:** Klaravex heading typeface (per brand assets in `brand/`), sentence-case, no all-caps.
- **Layout:** 1:1 (LinkedIn feed) + 1.91:1 (LinkedIn feed alternate). Both required.
- **Voice policy:** third-person corporate. No stock cliches ("shaking hands over graphs", "person pointing at laptop while colleague nods"). Real workflow moments only.
- **Trust signals:** subtle, not intrusive. Klaravex logo lower-right at 60% opacity. Tagline `Clarity. Security. Results.` under logo, tiny.
- **Do not use:** stock cybersecurity imagery (hooded figure at keyboard, glowing padlock, binary-code rain). Modern, honest workspaces only.

## Part 7 — Testing plan (14 days)

Week 1: 3 vertical sets + 1 retargeting + Conversation Ad live. No optimization — data gathering.

Week 2: shift budget by CTR-per-CPL:
- Any set below CTR target for 7 days → pause, iterate creative.
- Any set beating CPL target → +50% budget.
- Conversation Ad reply rate <2% → rewrite opening message.

Week 3+: scale winners, retire losers, refresh creative (LinkedIn creative fatigue kicks in fast — plan a fresh image every 3-4 weeks).
