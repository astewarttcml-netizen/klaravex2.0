### Page 1 — How to use this checklist

> This checklist maps a regulated SMB's security posture across 12 domains. Each item is a yes/no question a regulator, auditor, or client's procurement office might ask.
>
> **Green** (all items yes): you're likely audit-ready in that domain.
> **Yellow** (partial): you have controls but they aren't documented or tested.
> **Red** (mostly no): a competent adversary — or auditor — will exploit this.
>
> This is not legal or accounting advice. It is an operational checklist based on
> HIPAA Security Rule, SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria, ISO/IEC 27001:2022,
> and the FTC Safeguards Rule (2023 update).

### Pages 2-9 — The 12 domains (one page per 1-2 domains)

Each domain: 4-6 checklist items + 1-2 sentence "why this matters" note.

**Domain 1: Governance & policy**
- [ ] Written information security program, dated within 12 months
- [ ] Named security leader (internal or fractional CISO) with authority to enforce
- [ ] Risk assessment completed within 12 months, mapped to identified assets
- [ ] Board or ownership signed off on residual risk

**Domain 2: Asset & data inventory**
- [ ] Inventory of endpoints, servers, and cloud tenants — dated within 90 days
- [ ] Data classification: what qualifies as PHI, PII, or client-confidential
- [ ] Data flow diagram: where regulated data enters, lives, leaves

**Domain 3: Access control & identity**
- [ ] MFA enforced on every user account with email or file access
- [ ] Privileged access reviewed quarterly, principle-of-least-privilege documented
- [ ] Off-boarding process removes access within 4 hours of separation

**Domain 4: Endpoint & network defense**
- [ ] EDR (not just antivirus) on every endpoint, with a real 24/7 monitoring escalation path
- [ ] Patch cadence documented; critical CVEs deployed within 14 days
- [ ] Network segmentation between client-facing and administrative systems
- [ ] DNS filtering + email security gateway with active threat blocking

**Domain 5: Backup & recovery**
- [ ] Immutable backups covering every regulated data store
- [ ] Restore test performed within the last 90 days, with a written result
- [ ] Documented recovery time and recovery point objectives

**Domain 6: Vendor & third-party risk**
- [ ] Vendor inventory with data-handling classification for each
- [ ] Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) executed for every HIPAA vendor
- [ ] Annual review of critical vendors' SOC 2 reports (or equivalent)

**Domain 7: Incident response**
- [ ] Written incident response plan naming roles and phone numbers
- [ ] Tabletop exercise performed within 12 months, with documented gaps + fixes
- [ ] 24/7 escalation path to a competent responder (internal or partner)

**Domain 8: Training & phishing**
- [ ] Annual security awareness training completed by every user
- [ ] Quarterly phishing simulation with results tracked
- [ ] New-hire security orientation within first 30 days

**Domain 9: Encryption & data protection**
- [ ] Full-disk encryption on every endpoint
- [ ] TLS 1.2+ everywhere; internal TLS certificates managed
- [ ] Cloud storage encryption at rest verified per tenant

**Domain 10: Physical & remote work**
- [ ] Office access controlled; visitor log maintained
- [ ] Documented remote-work security policy
- [ ] Company-managed devices for regulated data; BYOD contained to non-regulated flows

**Domain 11: Monitoring & audit**
- [ ] Central log collection covering identity, endpoint, cloud, and email
- [ ] Alerts routed to a human who acts within one hour, 24/7
- [ ] Log retention meets your longest regulatory requirement

**Domain 12: Framework mapping & evidence**
- [ ] Every control mapped to at least one regulatory framework (HIPAA / SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / FTC Safeguards)
- [ ] Evidence repository with dated screenshots, policies, and test results
- [ ] Client security questionnaires answered within 5 business days

### Page 10 — Score yourself

- 40-48 checkmarks: audit-ready
- 30-39: real program but gaps
- 20-29: tools without a program
- 0-19: exposure — a single incident becomes existential
