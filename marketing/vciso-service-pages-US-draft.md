# Page 1 — vCISO Services for Small Law Firms
**Suggested URL:** `klaravex.com/vciso/law-firms`
**Target buyer:** Managing partner / firm administrator at a 2–50-attorney firm
**Primary intent:** "vCISO for law firms," "law firm cybersecurity," "ABA cybersecurity requirements"

---

### Your clients trust you with their most sensitive matters. Your IT should earn that same trust.

A single exposed matter file, a wired-funds fraud, or a ransomware lockout doesn't just cost money — it triggers ethics exposure under ABA Model Rules 1.1 and 1.6, client notification obligations, and a malpractice conversation no partner wants to have. Most small firms don't have a security leader. We become yours.

**Klaravex provides a virtual CISO (vCISO) for law firms** — a named security advisor who owns your risk posture, plus the managed security and monitoring to back it up. Not software you're left to run alone. A delivered service.

### What a Klaravex vCISO does for your firm

- **Owns your security posture.** Annual risk assessment, written information security program, and a prioritized remediation roadmap your partners can actually read.
- **Maps to the rules that govern you.** ABA Model Rules 1.1 (competence) and 1.6(c) (reasonable safeguards), the guidance in ABA Formal Opinion 498 on remote and cloud practice, and your state's breach-notification and data-protection statutes.
- **Defends against the attacks aimed at firms.** Business email compromise and trust-account wire fraud, ransomware, and credential theft — monitored and responded to, not just alerted on.
- **Speaks to your clients' security questionnaires.** When corporate clients send an outside-counsel security audit, we answer it — and make sure the answers are true.
- **Hardens the stack you already run.** Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, your document management system, remote access, and your firewall and network (Ubiquiti UniFi, included in every tier).

### Why a vCISO instead of "we'll just get antivirus"

Security tools tell you something is wrong. They don't decide what to do, prepare you for a client audit, or sit across from you when an incident hits. A vCISO does. For firms that handle confidential matters but can't justify a full-time security hire, this is the model that fits.

### Where this sits in our service tiers

vCISO is the core of our **Directive tier** — readiness advisory, managed detection and response, and security leadership in one engagement. Firms that need foundational managed IT first can start at **Foundation** or **Assurance** and add the vCISO layer when they're ready.

> **Talk to us about a vCISO for your firm.** Book a readiness conversation at **klaravex.com** — we'll walk your current posture and show you exactly where the gaps are.

### HIPAA names a Security Official. Most small practices have a checkbox, not a person.

The HIPAA Security Rule requires your practice to designate someone responsible for your security program. On paper, that's often the office manager. In reality, nobody owns it — until an OCR investigation, a vendor breach, or a ransomware event makes it everyone's problem at once.

**Klaravex serves as your outsourced security leadership — a virtual CISO (vCISO) for your practice** — and delivers the monitoring, response, and readiness work behind it. Documentation alone doesn't secure a practice. We pair the program with the protection.

### What a Klaravex vCISO does for your practice

- **Acts as your security program owner.** HIPAA Security Risk Analysis, written policies and safeguards, workforce training oversight, and an audit-ready evidence trail.
- **Prepares you for what's in effect — and what's coming.** Your obligations under the current HIPAA Security Rule today, and how to prepare for the **proposed 2026 Security Rule update** (mandatory encryption, multi-factor authentication, and faster incident reporting are in the proposal — not yet final, but the direction is clear).
- **Manages your Business Associate Agreements.** Every cloud tool that touches PHI needs a BAA. We track them so a vendor gap doesn't become your liability.
- **Detects and responds to threats.** Managed detection and response across endpoints, email, and your network — so a phishing click doesn't become a reportable breach.
- **Secures your everyday stack.** Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, your EHR connectivity, devices, and your firewall and network (Ubiquiti UniFi, included in every tier).

### Software gives you a seal. We give you a posture.

Compliance software can document your program and hand you a badge. It can't watch your network at 2am, contain an incident, or represent real audit-readiness when OCR asks for evidence. A vCISO-led managed service does both: the readiness work *and* the active defense.

Most advisory and accounting firms have none of this formalized.

**Klaravex provides a virtual CISO (vCISO) for financial advisory and accounting firms** — the security leader your examiners expect, plus the managed monitoring and response to make the program real.

### What a Klaravex vCISO does for your firm

- **Builds and owns your written information security program.** Risk assessment, documented safeguards, and a roadmap mapped to your regulator — SEC, FINRA, or the FTC Safeguards Rule.
- **Closes the gaps examiners probe.** Access controls, multi-factor authentication, encryption, vendor due diligence, and an incident response plan you can actually execute.
- **Manages third-party and vendor risk.** Your custodian, your tax or portfolio software, your cloud storage — each is an exposure point we assess and monitor.
- **Detects and responds to fraud and intrusion.** Managed detection and response tuned to the wire-fraud and account-takeover attacks aimed at firms that move client money.
- **Secures your core stack.** Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, your advisory or accounting platforms, devices, and your firewall and network (Ubiquiti UniFi, included in every tier).
