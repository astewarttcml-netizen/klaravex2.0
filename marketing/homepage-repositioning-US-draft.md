# Homepage Repositioning — Proposal (klaravex.com, US)

**Why this changes:** The competitive brief found an open quadrant no competitor holds — *compliance-readiness-led + delivered as an accountable managed service + vertical*. Platforms (Coro, Cynomi, Compliancy Group, Electric) ship a tool and offload delivery; Ntiva buries compliance under CMMC/GovCon. This homepage claims that quadrant explicitly.

**Decision required:** adopt / adjust / reject. This is a positioning reset, not a copy tweak — approve the thesis before the copy.

---

## Proposed thesis (one sentence)

> **Klaravex makes regulated small businesses audit-ready — and keeps them defended — as one accountable service, not a box of tools you're left to run alone.**

---

## A) Hero

**Headline:**
> Security and compliance readiness, delivered.

**Subhead:**
> Most security vendors hand you software and walk away. Klaravex gives healthcare, legal, and financial firms a named security leader, audit-readiness, and 24/7 detection and response — as one managed service.

**Primary CTA:** Book a readiness review
**Secondary CTA:** Take the 2-minute readiness self-assessment *(links to the self-assessment tool)*

---

## B) The problem (status quo we position against)

> **A tool isn't a security program.**
> You can buy endpoint software, a compliance portal, and a password manager — and still have no one who owns your risk, prepares you for an audit, or answers the phone when something breaks. Regulated firms don't need more tools. They need someone accountable.

---

## C) What we do — the three things, one service

| | |
|---|---|
| **Readiness advisory** | Risk assessment, written security program, and an audit-ready roadmap mapped to HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, or the FTC Safeguards Rule. |
| **Managed detection & response** | We watch your endpoints, email, and network — and act when something happens, instead of just sending you an alert. |
| **Named security leadership (vCISO)** | A real security advisor who owns your posture, answers your clients' questionnaires, and sits with you when it counts. |

> One team. One relationship. One bill. Your firewall and network management are included in every tier.

---

## D) Who we serve (vertical proof)

> **Built for the firms regulators actually scrutinize.**
> - **Medical & dental practices** — HIPAA readiness and the proposed 2026 Security Rule changes, handled.
> - **Law firms** — ABA-aligned safeguards and the client security audits your corporate clients send.
> - **Financial & accounting firms** — SEC/FINRA expectations and the FTC Safeguards Rule, met with evidence.

---

## E) Why Klaravex (differentiation block)

> **Delivered, not DIY.** Compliance software gives you a checklist and a seal. We give you a program, a defender, and proof.
> **Specialists, not generalists.** We work in healthcare, legal, and financial — not everything for everyone.
> **One accountable partner.** No finger-pointing between your "IT person," your "security tool," and your "compliance portal." It's us.

---

## F) Tiers (names public, Directive pricing gated)

> **Foundation** — managed IT and core security to run on.
> **Assurance** — adds deeper monitoring and security operations.
> **Directive** — readiness advisory + MDR + vCISO, our complete program for regulated firms.
>
> *Most regulated firms start with a readiness review, then we recommend the right tier.* **Request Directive pricing.**

---

## G) Closing CTA

> **Find your gaps before an auditor — or an attacker — does.**
> Book a readiness review at **klaravex.com**, or take the 2-minute self-assessment to see where you stand today.
