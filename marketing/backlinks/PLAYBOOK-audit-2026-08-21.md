# Klaravex Backlink & Citation Acquisition Playbook

Created: 2026-08-21. Owner: marketing. Scope: klaravex.com + personal.klaravex.com (US only — nothing here touches .de).

Why this exists: the 2026-06-11 SEO/AI-visibility audit scored brand-wide AI visibility **12/100** — Klaravex exists only on its own domains. AI assistants and search engines recommend MSPs from third-party signals: directories, review platforms, community mentions, and entity databases. No amount of on-site work fixes that; this playbook does.

**Hard rules for every listing and pitch:**
- Identical NAP everywhere, verbatim (from the audit):
  > **Klaravex LLC** · (424) 348-6010 · hello@klaravex.com · https://klaravex.com · Managed IT & security for US small businesses — AI-powered first-line support with senior engineers. HIPAA, SOC 2 and NIST CSF readiness advisory. Remote, nationwide.
- Corporation voice only ("we"/"Klaravex"), no personal names, no "Loki", no vendor names (Hetzner/Atera/etc.).
- The word **"compliance" never appears** in any listing or pitch copy — use "readiness" / "advisory".
- Same concrete numbers everywhere: 89% AI resolution, resolution in under six minutes, tier pricing. Consistency is what AI models extract.
- Every profile links klaravex.com (business) or personal.klaravex.com (consumer). No defense/DIB/CMMC positioning anywhere.
- **Gate:** the audit's P0 on-site defects (placeholder "$X" pricing, dead CTA, broken nav) must be fixed before driving reviewers to the site. Verify before submitting anywhere.
