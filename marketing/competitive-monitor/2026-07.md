## 3. Recommended Response

**Ntiva:** Their GRC blog is building content authority fast. Klaravex should publish a differentiated counter-piece: "GRC Infrastructure vs. GRC Accountability — Why a Platform Is Not an Outcome." Target the same MSP and SMB search terms but lead with the accountability angle (we sign off on your readiness, not just document it).

**Compliancy Group + Healthicity:** No action required this cycle. Set a watch for any security partnership announcement or move into legal/financial verticals — that is the risk scenario.

**Cynomi:** Their June 24 release narrows the tooling gap for their MSP channel partners. Klaravex should accelerate messaging around the "accountable delivery" differentiator — Cynomi gives MSPs a tool; Klaravex delivers the outcome and carries the responsibility. This is the clean wedge.

**Coro:** No action. Their AI-automated SOC bet is a positioning gift — it is the opposite of human-accountable MDR. Use their messaging in objection-handling scripts ("AI resolves your alerts, but who escalates to your team at 2am?").

**Electric:** No action. Continue monitoring for any compliance pivot; that would be a meaningful signal.
