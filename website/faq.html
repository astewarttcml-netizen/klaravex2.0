Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions prospects actually ask before signing.

The questions below show up on every discovery call. They are answered here so prospects can self-qualify before writing to hello@klaravex.com — and so existing clients have a stable reference for what is, and is not, in scope.

What does Klaravex actually do?

Klaravex LLC is a senior-principal managed-security and regulatory-readiness advisory practice for US small and mid-sized businesses (10–250 employees). Engagements combine ongoing managed security (identity, endpoint, email, network), HIPAA / SOC 2 / ISO 27001 readiness advisory, AI adoption guidance, and Ubiquiti UniFi network management. We operate at the senior-principal level on retainer; we do not staff junior tier-one support.

Who is a good fit for Klaravex?

US small and mid-sized businesses, typically 10 to 250 employees, running Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or AWS — with no internal CISO and no security team large enough to justify hiring one. Healthcare-adjacent organisations (HIPAA), legal and financial firms (PCI-DSS, GLB Safeguards), and SaaS SMBs preparing for SOC 2 are core verticals. EU-based organisations can engage Klaravex LLC under a written GDPR Data Processing Addendum.

How do the Foundation, Assurance, and Directive tiers differ?

Foundation ($100 per user per month) covers managed identity, endpoint, email security, and UniFi network management. Assurance ($165) adds 24/7 MDR, vulnerability management, and quarterly readiness reviews. Directive ($295) adds vCISO advisory and end-to-end HIPAA / SOC 2 / ISO 27001 readiness preparation. Directive is the tier we lead with for regulated SMBs; Foundation is the delivery floor, not the recommended pitch. The full tier breakdown lives on the service tiers page.

Do you issue compliance certifications?

No. Klaravex provides readiness advisory and preparation — gap analysis, control implementation, evidence collection, policy authorship, and audit-ready documentation. The actual SOC 2 attestation, ISO 27001 certification, HIPAA compliance posture validation, or PCI-DSS QSA review must be issued by an independent third-party auditor or assessor. We prepare you for that engagement; we do not perform it.

Do you serve US defense contractors, DIB, or CMMC engagements?

No. US defense, Defense Industrial Base, and CMMC engagements are explicitly out of scope. The ITAR export-control route was reviewed and declined in May 2026. Please refer those inquiries to a specialised defense-focused practice. Re-opening this market would require export-controls counsel and is not on the roadmap.

Can EU-based organisations engage Klaravex?

Yes — under a written GDPR Data Processing Addendum. Klaravex LLC is a Wyoming entity and acts as a US data processor in that arrangement. The engagement principal is based in Berlin, which makes time-zone-aligned coverage of US East Coast morning hours and EU business hours practical from a single workstation.

How quickly can an engagement start?

From signed engagement letter to first deliverable: typically two to four weeks. The path runs through discovery call (within one US business day of email), scoping conversation, written proposal, master services agreement and engagement letter signature, kickoff workshop, and the first sprint of work. Urgent incident-response work is handled separately and is not gated on the same paperwork — write to security@klaravex.com if you have an active incident.

What does the Ubiquiti UniFi network management include?

Every Klaravex service tier — Foundation, Assurance, and Directive — includes Ubiquiti UniFi firewall and network infrastructure management. That covers initial gateway and access-point provisioning, VLAN segmentation aligned to identity tiers, firewall rule maintenance, firmware update cycles, captive-portal configuration for guest networks, and monitoring of network anomalies. We standardise on UniFi because it delivers managed-network economics at SMB price points without sacrificing observability.

What is Klaravex AI and how is it used during an engagement?

Klaravex AI is our AI-native first-line support agent. For client-support inquiries, Klaravex AI handles initial contact, diagnosis, guided self-service, and common fixes; complex issues escalate directly to the engagement principal. Klaravex AI is the differentiator that lets a senior-principal practice deliver SMB-scale support economics without sacrificing response quality. Klaravex AI access is delivered as part of every managed engagement.

How do I start a conversation?

Write to hello@klaravex.com with your headcount, your primary identity platform (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or hybrid), any regulatory drivers (HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001), and your rough timeline. First response is typically within one US business day. For coordinated vulnerability disclosure affecting Klaravex infrastructure, write to security@klaravex.com — see RFC 9116 security.txt for the machine-readable contact policy. The full contact page documents all three lanes.
