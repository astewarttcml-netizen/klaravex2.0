How to choose

Start with Directive if your business is in healthcare-adjacent, legal, financial, or any sector where security and regulatory readiness are contractual prerequisites. Start with Assurance if you have had an incident or your insurer is asking detailed questions. Start with Foundation only if you are moving off break/fix and have no immediate regulatory or insurance pressure.

Scope disclaimer

All Klaravex service tiers are managed services and readiness advisory. We do not conduct certification, attestation, or audit — independent assessors perform that work. Readiness advisory prepares an organisation for an assessor; it does not substitute for the assessor's independent opinion. Managed services reduce, but do not eliminate, operational risk. Every engagement is governed by a written master services agreement that defines the scope and the limits. Klaravex LLC carries E&O insurance.
