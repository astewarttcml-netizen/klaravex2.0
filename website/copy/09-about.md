## SECTION 1: WHAT WE ARE

**Headline:** About Klaravex

Klaravex is an AI-powered managed IT and security advisory firm serving US and EU small and mid-sized businesses — and, through a dedicated consumer segment, individuals who need reliable IT help at home.

The premise is simple: most businesses and individuals don't need a large team. They need fast, senior-level support that doesn't disappear when things get complicated. Klaravex AI handles first-line triage and common issues instantly, 24/7. For anything complex — security decisions, regulatory advisory, incident response — a senior engineer takes over with full context in hand.

Klaravex LLC is a Wyoming limited liability company. The team operates across both the US and EU, which means both regulatory environments are covered and both markets are served from day one.

## SECTION 2: HOW WE WORK

**Section headline:** How we work

Every Klaravex engagement runs on the same model: Klaravex AI first, human expert when it matters.

Klaravex AI — Klaravex's named AI agent — is purpose-built for managed IT and security delivery. It triages issues, resolves common problems instantly, gathers context before escalating, and routes complex issues without delay. This isn't a ticket queue — it's a live, intelligent first layer.

When escalation is needed, a senior engineer steps in. Every complex issue — security incidents, framework advisory, configuration decisions, readiness programs — is handled by someone with direct production experience in the same environments your business runs on.

We work in structured engagements with defined deliverables and written Statements of Work. Platforms covered: Microsoft 365 and Azure, Google Workspace, Amazon Web Services — and Ubiquiti UniFi network infrastructure. We meet clients on the platforms they already use.
