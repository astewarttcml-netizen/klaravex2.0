This Business Associate Agreement (the "BAA") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} between **{{COVERED_ENTITY_NAME}}** ("Covered Entity") and **Klaravex LLC** ("Business Associate"), and supplements the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated {{MSA_DATE}} ("Underlying Agreement"). To the extent of any conflict between this BAA and the Underlying Agreement regarding the use or disclosure of Protected Health Information, this BAA controls.

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning set forth in 45 CFR §§ 160.103, 164.304, and 164.501, including: "Breach," "Designated Record Set," "Electronic Protected Health Information" ("ePHI"), "Health Care Operations," "Individual," "Privacy Rule," "Protected Health Information" ("PHI"), "Required by Law," "Secretary," "Security Rule," "Subcontractor," and "Unsecured Protected Health Information."

## 2. Permitted Uses and Disclosures of PHI

2.1 **Services.** Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the services described in the Underlying Agreement, or as Required by Law.

2.2 **Management and administration.** Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration, and to carry out its legal responsibilities, provided that any disclosure is Required by Law or (a) the recipient agrees in writing to protect the PHI and to notify Business Associate of breaches, and (b) the disclosure is necessary for the purpose.

2.3 **Data aggregation.** Business Associate may provide data aggregation services as defined at 45 CFR § 164.501 relating to the Health Care Operations of Covered Entity.

2.4 **De-identification.** Business Associate may de-identify PHI in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.514(b) and use the de-identified data for its own purposes, including service improvement.

2.5 **Minimum necessary.** Business Associate will limit its requests for, uses of, and disclosures of PHI to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose, consistent with 45 CFR § 164.502(b).

## 3. Obligations of Business Associate

3.1 **No further use or disclosure.** Business Associate will not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted by this BAA, the Underlying Agreement, or as Required by Law.

3.2 **Safeguards.** Business Associate will implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards that reasonably and appropriately protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of ePHI, in compliance with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164.

3.3 **Mitigation.** Business Associate will mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect of a use or disclosure of PHI in violation of this BAA.

3.4 **Reporting of unauthorized use or disclosure.** Business Associate will report to Covered Entity, in writing, any use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by this BAA, any Security Incident as defined in 45 CFR § 164.304, and any Breach of Unsecured PHI in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.410.

3.5 **Breach notification timing.** Business Associate will notify Covered Entity without unreasonable delay and in no case later than thirty (30) calendar days after Discovery of a Breach of Unsecured PHI, with the content required by 45 CFR § 164.410(c).

3.6 **Routine Security Incidents.** Unsuccessful Security Incidents that do not result in unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction of ePHI (e.g., pings, port scans, denied logins) are reported on an aggregated quarterly basis.

3.7 **Subcontractors.** Business Associate will require any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on its behalf to agree in writing to the same restrictions and conditions that apply to Business Associate under this BAA, per 45 CFR § 164.502(e)(1)(ii). A current list of approved Subcontractors is available on request.

3.8 **Access to PHI.** Business Associate will make PHI in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity within fifteen (15) business days of a written request, to allow Covered Entity to comply with 45 CFR § 164.524.

3.9 **Amendment of PHI.** Business Associate will make PHI in a Designated Record Set available for amendment and incorporate amendments per 45 CFR § 164.526, within thirty (30) days of a written request.

3.10 **Accounting of disclosures.** Business Associate will make available the information required to provide an accounting of disclosures in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.528 within thirty (30) days of a written request.

3.11 **Access to books and records.** Business Associate will make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary for purposes of determining Covered Entity's compliance.

## 4. Obligations of Covered Entity

4.1 **Notice of Privacy Practices.** Covered Entity will provide Business Associate with the current notice of privacy practices and any changes that affect Business Associate's permitted uses or disclosures.

4.2 **Restrictions.** Covered Entity will notify Business Associate of any restriction on the use or disclosure of PHI agreed to under 45 CFR § 164.522 that may affect Business Associate's performance.

4.3 **Permissible requests.** Covered Entity will not request Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under the Privacy Rule if done by Covered Entity, except as permitted by Section 2 above.

## 5. Term and Termination

5.1 **Term.** This BAA is effective on the Effective Date and continues until the Underlying Agreement terminates, unless terminated earlier under this Section 5.

5.2 **Termination for breach.** If a Party knows of a material breach by the other Party, the non-breaching Party will (a) provide written notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure (not less than thirty (30) days), and (b) if cure is not made, terminate this BAA and the Underlying Agreement.

5.3 **Return or destruction of PHI.** Upon termination, Business Associate will return or destroy all PHI received from, or created or received on behalf of, Covered Entity. If return or destruction is not feasible, Business Associate will extend the protections of this BAA to such PHI and limit further use and disclosure to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, for as long as Business Associate maintains the PHI.

## 6. Miscellaneous

6.1 **Regulatory references.** Any reference in this BAA to a section of HIPAA includes any subsequent amendment or successor provision.

6.2 **Amendment.** The Parties will negotiate in good faith to amend this BAA as necessary to comply with HIPAA, the HITECH Act, and subsequent regulations.

6.3 **Interpretation.** Ambiguities are resolved in favor of an interpretation that permits Covered Entity to comply with HIPAA.

6.4 **No third-party beneficiaries.** This BAA does not create rights in any third party.

6.5 **Survival.** Sections 3.4, 3.5, 3.8–3.11, 5.3, and 6.4 survive termination.
