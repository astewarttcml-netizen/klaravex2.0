## 1. Permitted Uses of Loki AI

Loki is Klaravex's AI-powered first-line support assistant. Permitted uses include:

1.1 **IT support inquiries** — asking questions about device issues, software configuration, network connectivity, account access, and common IT troubleshooting.

1.2 **Security guidance** — requesting best-practice guidance on password management, multi-factor authentication, phishing awareness, and general security hygiene.

1.3 **Service information** — inquiring about Klaravex service tiers, pricing, onboarding, and scheduling.

1.4 **Readiness advisory questions** — asking general questions about HIPAA readiness, SOC 2 preparation, ISO 27001 gap assessment, or Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace security configuration.

1.5 **Escalation requests** — requesting escalation to a senior engineer or requesting a scheduled remote support session.

1.6 **Document and resume assistance** (consumer services) — submitting resume content for review, requesting career or tech-onboarding guidance.

## 2. Prohibited Uses

The following uses of Loki AI, klaravex.com, and all Klaravex services are strictly prohibited:

2.1 **Unauthorized access attempts** — using any Klaravex service to attempt unauthorized access to third-party systems, networks, accounts, or data.

2.2 **Malicious content** — transmitting, uploading, or requesting generation of malware, ransomware, exploit code, phishing templates, or other malicious content.

2.3 **Deceptive identity** — misrepresenting your identity, organizational affiliation, or authority in any interaction with Loki or Klaravex personnel.

2.4 **Harmful or illegal content** — requesting or transmitting content that is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, threatening, obscene, or that violates the privacy rights of any individual.

2.5 **Automated scraping** — using automated tools, bots, or scripts to scrape, harvest, or systematically extract content from klaravex.com without prior written consent.

2.6 **Denial-of-service** — attempting to overload, disrupt, or degrade the performance of klaravex.com or the Loki AI backend through excessive requests or denial-of-service techniques.

2.7 **Medical or legal advice requests** — attempting to use Loki as a substitute for licensed medical, legal, financial, or regulatory counsel. Loki provides readiness advisory and IT support only.

2.8 **Sensitive personal data** — submitting Social Security numbers, full payment card numbers, health records (PHI), passwords, private keys, or similarly sensitive data through the chat widget or contact forms. Use secure, appropriate channels for such data.

2.9 **Competitive intelligence extraction** — using Loki interactions to systematically extract proprietary methodologies, pricing structures, or business processes for competitive purposes.

2.10 **Resale or redistribution** — reselling, sublicensing, or redistributing Klaravex services or AI outputs without express written authorization.

## 3. Data Handling

3.1 **Data collected during interactions** — Loki AI interactions (chat messages, submitted text) may be logged for quality assurance, service improvement, and support escalation purposes. See the Klaravex Privacy Policy at klaravex.com/privacy-policy/ for full details.

3.2 **No PHI on Klaravex infrastructure** — Klaravex's Loki AI backend operates on Hetzner VPS infrastructure, which is not HIPAA-eligible. Do not submit protected health information (PHI) through any Klaravex channel. HIPAA readiness advisory engagements involve scoping questions only — no PHI is transmitted or stored.

3.3 **Retention** — Support interaction data is retained for the duration of the client relationship plus three years, unless a shorter retention period is required by applicable law or requested by the client in writing.

3.4 **EU/GDPR clients** — EU clients and clients whose data subjects are EU residents are served under a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA). Contact hello@klaravex.com to request the DPA before submitting any personal data of EU residents.

3.5 **CCPA** — California residents have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale of personal information. Klaravex does not sell personal information. To exercise CCPA rights, contact hello@klaravex.com.

4.2 **Account security** — You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of any login credentials used to access the Klaravex client portal. Notify Klaravex immediately at support@klaravex.com if you suspect unauthorized access.

4.3 **Compliance with applicable law** — You agree to use Klaravex services only in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations in your jurisdiction, including but not limited to HIPAA (if applicable to your sector), CCPA, GDPR, and any applicable state privacy laws.

You are responsible for reviewing AI-generated recommendations before acting on them, particularly for security configurations, compliance preparations, and business-critical IT decisions.
