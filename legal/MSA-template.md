This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between **Klaravex LLC**, a Wyoming limited liability company with a registered office at {{KLARAVEX_ADDRESS}} ("Klaravex"), and **{{CLIENT_LEGAL_NAME}}**, a {{CLIENT_ENTITY_FORM}} with a principal place of business at {{CLIENT_ADDRESS}} ("Client"). Klaravex and Client are each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties."

## 1. Services

1.1 **Scope.** Klaravex will provide managed security, managed IT, advisory, and related professional services ("Services") to Client. The specific Services, deliverables, schedules, and fees for each engagement will be described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOWs") executed by both Parties and incorporated by reference into this Agreement.

1.2 **SOW precedence.** In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an SOW, this Agreement governs unless the SOW expressly references the section being modified.

1.3 **Change orders.** Material changes to scope, fees, or timeline of an active SOW require a written change order signed by both Parties.

## 2. Term and Termination

2.1 **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated under this Section 2. Each SOW has its own term as stated in that SOW.

2.2 **Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience on {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' written notice; Client remains liable for fees incurred through the effective date of termination.

2.3 **Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of written notice, or (b) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.

2.4 **Effect of termination.** Upon termination, Klaravex will (a) cease performance, (b) deliver completed work product paid for, and (c) return or destroy Client Confidential Information per Section 6.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 **Fees.** Fees are as stated in the applicable SOW. Recurring fees are invoiced monthly in advance; one-time fees are invoiced per the SOW.

3.2 **Payment terms.** Invoices are due Net 15 from invoice date unless an SOW states otherwise. Past due amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum permitted by law, whichever is less.

3.3 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of taxes; Client is responsible for all sales, use, and similar transactional taxes other than taxes on Klaravex's net income.

3.4 **Expenses.** Pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses are reimbursable at cost.

## 4. Intellectual Property

4.1 **Pre-existing IP.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing IP. No license is granted except as expressly stated.

4.2 **Deliverables.** Subject to full payment, Klaravex grants Client a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual license to use deliverables for Client's internal business operations.

4.3 **Klaravex tools.** Klaravex retains ownership of its templates, scripts, methodologies, runbooks, and AI agent configurations (including the Loki agent), even where used to produce a Deliverable.

4.4 **Feedback.** Client grants Klaravex a perpetual, royalty-free license to use feedback for product improvement, on a de-identified basis.

## 5. Warranties and Disclaimers

5.1 **Mutual warranties.** Each Party warrants it has authority to enter this Agreement and that performance will not violate any other obligation.

5.2 **Services warranty.** Klaravex warrants that Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards. Client's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is re-performance.

5.3 **DISCLAIMER.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND KLARAVEX DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT. KLARAVEX DOES NOT WARRANT THAT SERVICES WILL PREVENT ALL SECURITY INCIDENTS OR ACHIEVE ANY SPECIFIC COMPLIANCE OUTCOME.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 **Definition.** "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other, marked or reasonably understood to be confidential.

6.2 **Obligations.** The Receiving Party will (a) use Confidential Information solely to perform under this Agreement, (b) protect it with at least the same care as its own confidential information and no less than reasonable care, and (c) limit access to personnel and contractors with a need to know who are bound by equivalent obligations.

6.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that is (a) publicly available without breach, (b) independently developed without reference to it, (c) rightfully received from a third party without restriction, or (d) required to be disclosed by law (with prior notice where permitted).

6.4 **Survival.** Confidentiality obligations survive for five (5) years after termination, except trade secrets which are protected for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

## 7. Data Protection

7.1 **Data Processing Addendum.** Where Klaravex processes personal data on behalf of Client, the Parties will execute the Klaravex Data Processing Addendum (DPA), which is incorporated by reference into this Agreement.

7.2 **Protected Health Information (out of scope).** Klaravex's infrastructure is not configured for HIPAA-regulated workloads, and Services under this Agreement do not include the creation, receipt, maintenance, or transmission of Protected Health Information ("PHI") as defined under 45 CFR § 160.103. Client will not provide PHI to Klaravex, and Klaravex will not knowingly process PHI, under this Agreement. Any engagement requiring Klaravex to handle PHI is out of scope and would require a separate written agreement, HIPAA-eligible infrastructure, and an executed Business Associate Agreement before any PHI is exchanged.

7.3 **Security baseline.** Klaravex maintains the security measures described in `https://klaravex.com/legal/security`, updated from time to time.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 **EXCLUSION OF DAMAGES.** EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY ARISING FROM (a) BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY, (b) INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, (c) GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, OR (d) UNPAID FEES, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, PUNITIVE, OR EXEMPLARY DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS OR LOST DATA.

8.2 **CAP.** EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT TO KLARAVEX IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM. This cap does not apply to the exclusions in Section 8.1(a)-(d).

## 9. Indemnification

9.1 **By Klaravex.** Klaravex will defend and indemnify Client against third-party claims alleging that Klaravex's deliverables, as delivered and used in accordance with this Agreement, infringe a US patent, copyright, or trademark.

9.2 **By Client.** Client will defend and indemnify Klaravex against third-party claims arising from (a) Client data, (b) Client's use of deliverables outside the license granted, or (c) Client's instructions to Klaravex.

9.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party will (a) promptly notify the indemnifying Party, (b) give sole control of defense and settlement, and (c) cooperate at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party will not settle on terms that admit fault or impose obligation on the indemnified Party without consent.

## 10. Insurance

Prior to the commencement of Services under the first Statement of Work executed pursuant to this Agreement, and for the duration of any active SOW, Klaravex will maintain professional liability (technology errors and omissions) insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, and cyber liability insurance with a limit of at least $1,000,000. Certificates of insurance evidencing such coverage are available to Client on request.

## 11. Independent Contractor

The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, employment, or fiduciary relationship.

## 12. Non-Solicitation

During the term and for twelve (12) months after, neither Party will directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who was substantively involved in the Services, except via general public advertising.
