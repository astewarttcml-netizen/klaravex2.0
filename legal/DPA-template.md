## 1. Definitions

"GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and, where applicable, the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018. "CCPA" means the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA). "Personal Data," "Processing," "Data Subject," "Controller," "Processor," "Sub-processor," and "Supervisory Authority" have the meanings given in the GDPR. "Sell," "Share," "Service Provider," and "Personal Information" have the meanings given in the CCPA. "Standard Contractual Clauses" or "SCCs" means the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries adopted by the European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021. "UK IDTA" means the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the SCCs issued under the UK Data Protection Act 2018.

## 2. Roles and Scope

2.1 **Roles.** With respect to Controller Personal Data, Controller is the Controller and Processor is the Processor. For CCPA-regulated Personal Information, Controller is the Business and Processor is the Service Provider.

2.2 **Subject matter and duration.** Processor will Process Controller Personal Data for the duration of the Underlying Agreement, solely to provide the Services described therein.

2.3 **Nature and purpose.** Processing operations include collection, storage, retrieval, access, organization, structuring, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure, and destruction in connection with the Services.

2.4 **Types of Personal Data and categories of Data Subjects.** As set out in Annex A.

## 3. Processor Obligations

3.1 **Documented instructions.** Processor will Process Controller Personal Data only on documented instructions from Controller, including the Underlying Agreement and this DPA, except where Required by Law (in which case Processor will notify Controller before Processing, unless prohibited).

3.2 **Confidentiality.** Processor ensures that persons authorized to Process Controller Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

3.3 **Security.** Processor implements appropriate technical and organizational measures consistent with Article 32 GDPR, including pseudonymization and encryption where appropriate, integrity and availability of systems, restoration of availability after incidents, and regular testing of effectiveness. Current measures are described in Annex B.

3.4 **Sub-processors.** Controller authorizes Processor to engage the Sub-processors listed in Annex C. Processor will (a) notify Controller of any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-processor at least thirty (30) days in advance, giving Controller the opportunity to object on reasonable Data Protection grounds, (b) impose Data Protection obligations on Sub-processors no less protective than those in this DPA, and (c) remain liable for Sub-processor performance.

3.5 **Data Subject requests.** Taking the nature of Processing into account, Processor will assist Controller by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, in responding to Data Subject requests to exercise rights under Chapter III GDPR.

3.6 **Security incidents.** Processor will notify Controller without undue delay (and not later than seventy-two (72) hours) of becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach, providing the information required by Article 33(3) GDPR to the extent known.

3.7 **DPIA assistance.** Processor will provide reasonable assistance to Controller with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with Supervisory Authorities under Articles 35 and 36 GDPR.

3.8 **Return or deletion.** Upon termination of the Underlying Agreement, Processor will, at Controller's election, return or delete all Controller Personal Data, unless retention is Required by Law. Processor will certify deletion on request.

3.9 **Audits.** Once per twelve (12) month period, Controller may audit Processor's compliance with this DPA, on at least thirty (30) days' written notice, during normal business hours, subject to confidentiality and Processor's reasonable security policies. Processor may satisfy this obligation by providing a current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent independent assessment.

3.10 **Records.** Processor maintains records of Processing activities consistent with Article 30(2) GDPR and will make them available to Supervisory Authorities on request.

## 4. CCPA Service Provider Obligations

4.1 Processor is a Service Provider with respect to Personal Information disclosed by Controller. Processor will not (a) Sell or Share Personal Information, (b) retain, use, or disclose Personal Information for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in the Underlying Agreement, (c) retain, use, or disclose Personal Information outside the direct business relationship between the Parties, or (d) combine Personal Information received from Controller with Personal Information from other sources, except as permitted by 11 CCR § 7050(b).

4.2 Processor will provide Controller with the assistance necessary to comply with consumer requests under the CCPA.

## 5. International Data Transfers

5.1 **EU SCCs.** Where Processor Processes Controller Personal Data subject to GDPR outside the European Economic Area, the Parties agree that the SCCs (Module Two: Controller to Processor) apply and are incorporated by reference. Where Processor engages Sub-processors outside the EEA, the SCCs (Module Three: Processor to Processor) apply between Processor and the Sub-processor.

5.2 **UK transfers.** Where the UK GDPR applies, the UK IDTA is incorporated by reference and applies in addition to the SCCs.

5.3 **Operative SCC choices.** Optional Clause 7 (docking) applies. Option 2 of Clause 9(a) applies, with the notification period in Section 3.4 above. Clause 11(a) optional language is not selected. Clause 17 Option 1 applies; the governing law is Ireland unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing. Clause 18(b) venue is the courts of Ireland.

5.4 **Annexes.** The Annexes to this DPA serve as Annexes to the SCCs.

## 6. Liability

Liability arising from this DPA is subject to the limitations of liability in the Underlying Agreement, except where applicable law prohibits such limitation. Nothing in this DPA limits or excludes liability of either Party toward Data Subjects under Article 82 GDPR.

## 7. Term

This DPA is effective on the Effective Date and continues for the duration of the Underlying Agreement. Sections 3.6, 3.8, 3.9, 3.10, 4, 5, and 6 survive termination for as long as Processor (or any Sub-processor) Processes Controller Personal Data.

## Annex B — Security Measures

Processor maintains, at minimum:

- Documented information security program reviewed annually
- Access control with role-based authorization and least privilege
- Multi-factor authentication for all administrative and remote access
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent)
- Endpoint security with managed detection and response on production systems
- Centralized logging and monitoring with at least 12-month retention
- Vulnerability management with quarterly internal scans
- Documented incident response plan exercised annually
- Personnel background checks and security awareness training
- Business continuity and disaster recovery plan with tested backups
- Physical security via SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certified cloud providers
- Secure software development lifecycle with peer code review
