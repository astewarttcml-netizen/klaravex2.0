What are the conditions for BWC partners to participate in the 2026 summer campaign?

KM

BWC partners participating in the 2026 summer campaign must meet the following conditions:

  • Be onboarded under the 2026 BWC partner process1 — sustainability-track partners benefit from the shortened 14-working-day onboarding.
  • Confirm participation by 15 May 2026 via the partner portal1.
  • Offer at least one campaign-specific perk to BWC holders (minimum 10 % discount, or a value-add of equivalent visitor benefit).2
  • Display the official Summer Campaign 2026 badge in physical premises and online presence from 1 June to 30 September 20262.
  • For sustainability-track partners: maintain valid GreenSign Berlin certification through campaign period3 (currently under re-submission review — see source 3).

The campaign launches 1 June with marketing pushed through visitBerlin's owned channels and the eight partner travel-trade markets.2

Markets briefing: Nordics Q3 outlook

Markets · International tourism · Q3 forecast briefing for Nordic source markets.

Nordic-market demand for Berlin tourism is forecast to rise 14 % year-over-year for the summer 2026 period, driven by Swedish and Danish leisure travellers and a recovery in Norwegian MICE flows after a soft 2025.

New BWC partner activations in Q3 will favour sustainability-track operators — a deliberate alignment with the GreenSign Berlin certification programme and with northern-market traveller sentiment. Air-route capacity from Stockholm-Arlanda and Copenhagen has expanded ~9 % versus Q3 2025.

Risks: a) macroeconomic softening in Sweden may dampen leisure spend in late Q3; b) ferry-route disruption (Trelleborg–Rostock) is forecast to constrain Swedish car traffic by ~6 %.
