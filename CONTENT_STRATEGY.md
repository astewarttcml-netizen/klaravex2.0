# Klaravex Content Strategy

**Authority:** Aligns to `CLAUDE.md` (brand voice) and **klaravex.com**.
When this file and `CLAUDE.md` disagree, **`CLAUDE.md` wins**.

## Executive Summary

Position Klaravex as the AI-powered managed IT and security partner for US
SMBs that run lean: Klaravex AI on first-line work, senior engineers on the
rest, published tiers (**Foundation $49 · Assurance $79 · Directive $129**),
and the **full service catalog** on klaravex.com (four pillars: network &
security, strategy & vCIO, cloud & productivity including **Microsoft 365,
Google Workspace, Azure, and AWS**, infrastructure & support — plus MDR and
readiness advisory). **UniFi ≤5%** of content.

## Content Objectives

1. Generate qualified B2B leads for Foundation / Assurance / Directive
2. Rank and educate on vertical readiness (law, accounting, medical, advisors,
   real estate) without marketing the word “compliance”
3. Demonstrate AI-first delivery proof (89% AI resolution, 2hr senior SLA,
   $0 commissions)
4. Cover the **full** stack on the site — not a single-vendor story
5. Support B2C separately via personal.klaravex.com (home tech)

## Target Audience Segments

1. **Accounting / CPA / tax** — FTC Safeguards, client data, insurance
2. **Small law firms** — confidentiality, M365, SOC 2 / NIST alignment
3. **Medical / dental / healthcare-adjacent** — HIPAA readiness
4. **Financial advisory** — vCISO, regulated data
5. **Real estate & architecture** — lean ops, identity, backup
6. **Overloaded internal IT** — co-managed interest
7. **B2C households** (separate track) — Wi‑Fi, devices, phishing, backups

## Cadence Notes

- Prefer vertical-specific angles over generic “MSP tips”
- Name tiers only with official prices
- Pair enterprise firewall content with UniFi only when the audience is SMB
  office networking — never imply Klaravex only sells UniFi
- Ban “compliance” in marketing copy → readiness / advisory / audit-ready
