## CONTENT

### Google Workspace, run like an enterprise — built for how you actually work.

Startups, agencies, and nonprofits choose Google Workspace for a reason: it's fast, collaborative, and light to run. We make sure it's also **secure, audit-ready, and properly governed.**

### What's included

- Tenant setup & migration (from M365, on-prem email, or another GWS)
- Security hardening — 2SV/MFA enforcement, context-aware access, admin least-privilege
- Vault & eDiscovery for legal/records retention
- Gemini AI enablement — turn on Google's built-in AI safely
- Identity & SSO, device management, data-loss prevention

### Nonprofit note

Google Workspace is **free for qualifying 501(c)(3)s** (including Gemini and NotebookLM). We'll get you set up and help you claim it.
