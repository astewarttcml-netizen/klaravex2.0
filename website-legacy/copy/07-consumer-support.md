## SECTION 2: HOW REMOTE SUPPORT WORKS
*(Prominent section — must appear near top)*

**Section headline:** How remote support works

When you book a session — or when Klaravex AI escalates your issue to a real technician — your expert joins you remotely. Here's what that means in practice:

Your technician shares your screen with you (only with your permission). They can see what you see, diagnose the problem directly, and fix it — without needing to be in the room. Most issues that need a human are resolved in a single session of 30 minutes or less.

**What remote support covers:**
- Works on Windows and macOS computers
- Works on iPhone, iPad, and Android devices (for issues that can be addressed remotely)
- No home visit required
- Your session is private — we do not record without your explicit consent

**What remote support cannot cover:**
Physical hardware repair — cracked screens, liquid damage, dead batteries — requires a repair shop. If your device won't turn on, that's out of scope for remote support. We'll tell you honestly if that's the case, and won't charge you for a session we can't help with.

## SECTION 4: WHAT'S NOT COVERED
*(Gentle, honest — no fine print tone)*

**Section headline:** What we can't help with remotely

A few things are outside the scope of remote support — and we'd rather tell you upfront than waste your time and money:

- **Physical hardware repair** — cracked screens, liquid damage, broken components, or a device that won't turn on. These need a repair shop or manufacturer service.
- **Business IT and infrastructure** — server management, network infrastructure, and multi-user IT environments. Our [business plans](/business/) are designed for that.
- **Issues that genuinely require on-site presence** — these are rare, but if something truly cannot be resolved remotely, we'll tell you at the start of the session and not charge you for it.
