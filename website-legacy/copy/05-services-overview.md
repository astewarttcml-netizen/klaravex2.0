### TIER: DIRECTIVE
**Eyebrow:** Most comprehensive

**Headline:** Directive
**Price anchor:** $150–250 / user / month

**Who it's for:**
- Organizations with regulatory obligations — HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIS2, DORA — and no internal CISO or security team
- EU financial entities and operators subject to DORA and NIS2
- Healthcare-adjacent firms managing PHI as a covered entity or business associate
- SMBs that have outgrown reactive IT and need a documented security program

**What's included:**
- vCISO advisory — quarterly reviews, security roadmap, board-level reporting
- Klaravex AI — 24/7 first-line support with senior engineer escalation
- Managed detection and response (MDR) — continuous monitoring and threat response
- Readiness programs: HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIS2, DORA (as applicable)
- Policy and procedure development
- Incident response planning and tabletop facilitation
- M365/Azure, Google Workspace, and AWS security management
- Ubiquiti UniFi network and firewall management
- Risk register maintenance

**Platform coverage:** M365/Azure · Google Workspace · AWS · Ubiquiti UniFi

**What it is not:**
- Not a substitute for a licensed auditor or certification body — Klaravex does not issue certifications or attestations
- Not a legal compliance opinion — regulatory determinations require qualified counsel
