Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-06

Klaravex LLC ("Klaravex", "we", "us") provides managed security, regulatory-readiness advisory, and AI adoption services. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to visitors to klaravex.com, prospects, and clients in the United States and the European Economic Area.

1. Who we are

Klaravex LLC is a Wyoming, USA limited liability company. For privacy questions, contact hello@klaravex.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact data you submit (name, email, company, message).
  • Engagement data from active client work (account identifiers, ticket content, system telemetry strictly within an agreed scope of services).
  • Site usage data (request logs, device and browser metadata, referrer).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to inquiries and deliver contracted services.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the site and our service offerings.
  • To comply with legal, tax, and contractual obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use site data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. Legal bases (EEA visitors)

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: (a) performance of a contract, (b) our legitimate interests in operating and securing our services, (c) compliance with legal obligations, and (d) your consent where required.

5. Sharing

We share information only with vetted service providers acting as processors under written agreements (hosting, email delivery, payment processing, productivity tools), and with authorities where legally required. For EU client engagements, Klaravex LLC acts as a US- based data processor under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and applies the relevant EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

Inquiry data is retained up to 24 months. Client engagement records are retained for the duration of the engagement plus the period required by applicable law and our professional liability insurer.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or object to processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, and limit the use of sensitive personal information, and the right not to be retaliated against for exercising those rights. To exercise any right, email hello@klaravex.com.

"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" — Klaravex does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

8. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures consistent with our service offerings, including encryption in transit, least-privilege access, logging, and vendor due diligence. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected individuals and regulators where required by law in the event of a qualifying incident.

9. International transfers

Klaravex LLC is based in the United States. Where EU/EEA personal data is transferred to us, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and supplementary measures as appropriate.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, by direct notice.

11. Contact
