About Klaravex

A senior-principal managed-security practice — built for US SMBs that can't justify hiring a full security team.

Klaravex LLC is a Wyoming-registered managed-security and regulatory-readiness advisory practice. We work with US small and mid-sized businesses — typically 10 to 250 employees — that need senior-level security and Microsoft 365 expertise on retainer but cannot economically hire that talent in-house.

What we do

Three things, deliberately scoped:

  • Managed security operations for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and AWS environments — identity, endpoint, network, and tenant hardening, with Ubiquiti UniFi network management included on every tier.
  • Regulatory readiness advisory for HIPAA, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 — preparing the controls, evidence, and runbooks that allow a client to engage an external assessor with confidence. We do not conduct certification or attestation ourselves.
  • AI adoption — Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini for Workspace, and tenant-resident generative AI tooling — deployed with identity governance, data classification, and acceptable-use policy in place from day one.

Who we are not

  • We are not a generalist break/fix shop. Foundation tier is a delivery mechanism for clients moving off break/fix — it is not the reason to choose Klaravex.
  • We are not a certification body. We prepare organisations for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and HIPAA readiness — independent assessors and auditors handle attestation.
  • We do not serve US defense, DIB, or CMMC engagements. ITAR was reviewed and explicitly declined as a market. Inquiries are referred to specialised CMMC-focused practices.
  • We are not an offshore helpdesk. Tier-1 contact runs through Klaravex AI first-line support; Tier-2 and Tier-3 escalations are handled by the principal directly.

How engagements work

Every engagement is governed by a written master services agreement and an engagement letter that defines scope, deliverables, and limits. Discovery is a 30-minute conversation about your current environment and regulatory drivers — no pitch deck, no obligation. Foundation, Assurance, and Directive tiers are the standard service shapes; custom scope is available on request for organisations that fall outside the standard tiers.

Where we operate

US clients — the primary market. Billing in USD, governed under Wyoming law via the engagement MSA, served by Klaravex LLC as the direct contracting entity.

EU clients — served by Klaravex LLC as a US data processor under a written GDPR Data Processing Addendum. The principal is based in Berlin, which makes time-zone-aligned coverage of EU business hours practical. EU-specific sector regulation and German Datenschutz advisory are delivered through a separate EU practice and are out of scope for Klaravex LLC.

Insurance, compliance, and limits

Klaravex LLC carries E&O insurance with limits sized to the engagements we underwrite. Privacy practices are documented under CCPA, CVCDPA, and equivalent US state regimes; EU client data handling is governed by the engagement DPA. Coordinated vulnerability disclosure is published per RFC 9116.

Why "Klaravex"

From clara (clear) and vex (to engage with difficulty). Security and regulatory work involves engaging seriously with difficult problems — and producing clarity for the client at the end of it. That is the practice in one word.

Scope disclaimer

Klaravex LLC provides managed services and readiness advisory only. We do not conduct certification, attestation, or audit. Readiness advisory prepares an organisation for an external assessor — it does not substitute for that assessor's independent work. Managed services reduce, but do not eliminate, operational risk. Every engagement is governed by a written master services agreement that defines the scope and the limits.
