Contact

Reach Klaravex — one of three doors, depending on why you are writing.

Klaravex LLC keeps three contact lanes deliberately separate: prospective work, current-client support, and coordinated vulnerability disclosure. Choose the lane that matches your reason for writing — it routes faster.

Prospective engagements

For discovery calls, scoping conversations, and proposals across Foundation, Assurance, and Directive tiers — or custom-scoped work that falls outside the standard tiers.

Email: hello@klaravex.com

Typical first response: within one US business day.

What helps us scope quickly: headcount, primary identity platform (Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace / hybrid), regulatory drivers if any (HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001), and timeline.

Existing-client support

For clients with an active master services agreement. Klaravex AI first-line support handles initial triage; escalations route directly to the engagement principal.

Email: support@klaravex.com

Coverage hours: as defined in your engagement letter. Emergency escalation paths are documented in the runbook delivered at engagement kickoff.

Coordinated vulnerability disclosure

If you believe you have identified a security issue affecting Klaravex infrastructure, our marketing site, or a service we deliver, please write to the security contact. We follow coordinated disclosure principles and will work with you in good faith.

Email: security@klaravex.com

Machine-readable policy: RFC 9116 security.txt

Preferred languages: English, German.
